### Abstract

The 2023 Organ Procurement and Transplantation Network pediatric medical urgency update revised the Pediatric End-Stage Liver Disease (PELD) calculation, pediatric Status 1A/1B rules, and National Liver Review Board guidance. Using registry data linked to exception records, we performed an interrupted time-series analysis among candidates younger than 12 years listed from January 2019 through December 2024, excluding June-July 2023 as the implementation interval (2,791 registrations; 368,761 person-days).
