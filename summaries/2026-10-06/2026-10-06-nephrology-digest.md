### Abstract

Chronic graft-versus-host disease (cGVHD) is a significant cause of morbidity and mortality following allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplantation. During cGVHD, T follicular helper cells (TFH) and germinal center B-cells (GCB) interactions produce pathogenic class-switched allo- and auto-antibodies. We utilized a multi-organ cGVHD mouse model with bronchiolitis obliterans (BO) to investigate TFH metabolism and metabolic vulnerabilities at early and late times post-transplant. We found that TFH were highly glycolytic early in cGVHD/BO progression but later transitioned to a more oxidative metabolic profile.
