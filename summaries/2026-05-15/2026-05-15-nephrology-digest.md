### Abstract

Kidney transplantation is prioritized for children in the United States, yet equity for highly sensitized pediatric candidates remains uncertain. Using Organ Procurement and Transplantation Network data, we examined kidney‐only candidates aged 0–17 years listed between December 4, 2014 and December 31, 2024 who were ever activated, comparing the Kidney Allocation System (KAS; Era 1) and the 2021 circle-based revision (KAS250; Era 2). Candidates were stratified by calculated panel-reactive antibody (cPRA) at first activation (0–20%, 21–79%, 80–99%, 100%).
