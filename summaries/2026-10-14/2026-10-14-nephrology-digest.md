### Abstract

The Scientific Registry of Transplant Recipients(SRTR) is the primary source of national transplant data in the United States; however, advanced perfusion techniques, including normothermic regional perfusion(NRP) and normothermic machine perfusion(NMP), are not consistently captured.We performed a multicenter validation study comparing SRTR-derived algorithms with confirmed reference standard cases from Mayo Clinic Florida, Mayo Clinic Arizona, and Tampa General Hospital. Algorithms were developed to identify NRP, in-transit NMP, and back-to-base NMP using existing SRTR variables.
