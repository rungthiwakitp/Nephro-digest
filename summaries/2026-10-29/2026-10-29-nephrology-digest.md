### Abstract

Baseline lung allograft dysfunction (BLAD) contextualises peak post-transplant lung function, but the prognostic impact of GLI-2012 versus GLI-2022 and respective 80% predicted versus LLN classification remains unclear. In 1283 adult bilateral first lung transplant recipients from three European centres, baseline FEV1 was defined as the mean of the two highest post-transplant values at one year after transplantation or beyond, with corresponding FVC values. Percent-predicted values and LLN were calculated using GLI-2012 and GLI-2022, and subnormal lung function was evaluated for graft survival, CLAD-free survival, and survival after CLAD diagnosis using Kaplan–Meier and adjusted Cox models.

### Abstract

Long-term outcomes after kidney re-transplantation in patients with childhood-onset kidney replacement therapy (KRT) are poorly characterized. Using European Renal Association Registry data, we studied 12,623 patients who started KRT before age 20 years and received one (n = 11,078), two (n = 2,962) or three (n = 718) kidney transplants (KTs) between 1978 and 2019. In this descriptive study, 5-, 10- and 15-year patient survival was 97, 94 and 90 % after first, 97, 95, 94 % after second and 96, 94, 92 % after third KT; corresponding graft survival was 79, 63, 50 %, 69, 52, 38 %, and 67, 51, 38 %, respectively.
