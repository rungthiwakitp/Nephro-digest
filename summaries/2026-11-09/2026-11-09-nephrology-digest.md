- URL: [Article link](https://www.amjtransplant.org/article/S1600-6135(26)02887-X/fulltext?rss=yes)
- Matched keywords: kidney, transplant, proteinuria, IgA nephropathy

### Abstract

Recurrent crescentic IgA nephropathy (IgAN) after kidney transplantation is rare, aggressive, and often refractory to standard immunosuppression. We report a 17-year-old recipient with biopsy-confirmed recurrent crescentic IgAN two years after transplantation despite corticosteroids and targeted-release budesonide. Rescue therapy comprised one rituximab infusion with daratumumab, followed by four daratumumab infusions. Proteinuria decreased from 5.21 to 0.06 g/g, serum albumin normalized, and allograft function remained stable through day 482 after the first rescue infusion.

### Abstract

Post-donation follow-up is critical for living kidney donor (LKD) care, facilitating early detection of unusual decrease in renal function and providing opportunities for early intervention. Herein, we update and characterize temporal trends in LKD follow-up (LDF) using Organ Procurement and Transplantation Network data. We identified 64,488 LKDs (2/2013-12/2023) and used logistic regression to assess donor characteristics associated with complete and timely (defined per current OPTN policy) LDF at 6-months, 1-year and 2-years post-donation.

- Matched keywords: kidney, transplant

### Abstract

Early corticosteroid cessation is recommended for low immune risk kidney transplant recipients, but the optimal induction immunosuppression is uncertain. We compared the outcomes of 4,770 first kidney only transplant recipients in the U.S. between 2010 and 2022 treated with tacrolimus and mycophenolate without corticosteroids induced with interleukin-2 receptor antagonists (IL-2Ra) to a matched cohort induced with anti-thymocyte globulin rabbit ( Thymoglobulin.) Only 13% of patients treated with early corticosteroid cessation were induced with IL-2Ra and these patients were more likely to be older and have diabetes.
