### Abstract

Kidney allocation relies on rapid sequential offers to maximize organ utilization, yet patient-level refusal remains poorly characterized. We conducted a retrospective study of kidney refusal encounters across three transplant centers over two years. Among 841 deceased donor offers where patients were contacted, 108 resulted in patient-level refusals (13%). Donation after circulatory death (DCD) kidneys represented 51% of offers and 54% of refusals, whereas donation after brain death (DBD) kidneys comprised 49% of offers and 46% of refusals.
