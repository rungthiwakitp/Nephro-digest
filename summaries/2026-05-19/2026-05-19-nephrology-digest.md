### Abstract

The kidney transplant community is once again confronted with disappointing results from a rigorously conducted clinical trial in antibody-mediated rejection (AMR). IMAGINE, a global, phase 3, multicenter, randomized, placebo-controlled trial, evaluated clazakizumab, a direct interleukin-6 (IL-6) inhibitor for the treatment of chronic active AMR in kidney transplant recipients[1]. This trial was the first in the kidney transplant field to use change in mean eGFR from baseline to week 52 as a surrogate endpoint accepted by the US Food and Drug Administration, and investigators successfully enrolled 194 of a planned 350 participants, making IMAGINE the largest placebo-controlled AMR trial to date.
