### Abstract

Transient receptor potential cation channel subfamily V member 1+ (TRPV1+) sensory nerves innervate the kidneys. Outside the kidney, these are known to be pain-sensing nerves. Zhang et al. studied the function of these nerves in the kidneys. They found that the TRPV1+ nerves were activated by inflammation during experimental ischemia-reperfusion injury. Inflammation stimulated the nerves to release calcitonin gene-related peptide (CGRP), which mediated high expression of interleukin-4 receptor alpha on a subset of macrophages by signaling through macrophage receptor activity modifying protein 1 (RAMP1) receptors.
