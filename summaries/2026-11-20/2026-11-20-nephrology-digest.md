### Abstract

Proliferative glomerulonephritis with “monoclonal” immunoglobulin deposits (PGNMID) is a glomerular-limited disease characterized by glomerulonephritis with non-organized monotypic immunoglobulin deposits. Most cases are derived from IgG3, while the remainder are derived from IgG1, IgG2, IgA, IgM, or immunoglobulin light chain (LC) only. Despite the known low rates of detection of monoclonal gammopathy (MG) and B-cell clonal proliferative disorders in PGNMID (especially PGNMID-IgG3), it has traditionally been considered a monoclonal gammopathy of renal significance (MGRS) lesion.
