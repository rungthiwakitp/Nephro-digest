### Abstract

Ureteral stenosis is a frequent urological complication after kidney transplantation and a relevant cause of graft dysfunction, often requiring repeated interventions due to high rates of restenosis following conventional balloon dilatation. Strategies targeting the underlying fibrotic remodeling remain limited.We report a novel clinical application of paclitaxel-coated balloon ureteroplasty in a transplanted ureter. A kidney transplant recipient developed recurrent distal ureteral stenosis at the ureterovesical anastomosis, with hydronephrosis and graft dysfunction despite standard endourological management, including balloon dilatation and ureteral stenting, complicated by recurrent urinary tract infections.

### Abstract

Enteric leaks following pancreas transplant cause significant morbidity and may result in allograft loss. The proximal duodenal staple line is particularly susceptible. We describe a novel technique utilizing omentopexy to mitigate proximal duodenal staple line leaks. This was a single center, retrospective analysis of all pancreas transplants performed from 10/01/2014-7/31/2024. The omentopexy technique was introduced in 10/2019. A piece of donor omentum is mobilized and retained during procurement, then used to buttress the proximal duodenal staple line after implantation.
