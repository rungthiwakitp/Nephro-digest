### Abstract

Pancreas transplantation (PTX) is the single most effective therapeutic option to restore sustained euglycemia and insulin independence in patients with insulin-requiring diabetes mellitus; however, recurrence of hyperglycemia and diabetes mellitus after PTX may occur. In this personal viewpoint, we propose a practical diagnostic and management framework for post-transplant diabetes mellitus (PTDM), and its distinction from pancreas graft failure (PGF) and other causes of hyperglycemia following PTX.
