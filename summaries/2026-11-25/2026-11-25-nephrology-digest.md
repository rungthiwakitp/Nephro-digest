### Abstract

We report the case of a man with a lifelong history of chronic intestinal ulcerations and bleeding, later complicated by chronic kidney disease and bilateral kidney calcifications. Symptoms began in the neonatal period with abdominal pain, diarrhea, iron deficiency and duodenal ulcers. During adolescence, duodenal ulcerative stenosis required surgical vagotomy and partial gastrectomy. Endoscopic and histologic evaluation supported inflammatory bowel disease, although no granulomas were identified on biopsy.

### Abstract

Simultaneous kidney-pancreas transplantation is the most definitive treatment for type 1 diabetes with end-stage renal manifestations. While most kidney-pancreas transplants are performed via open approaches, select groups have begun performing multiport robotic transplants with and without hand assistance. Here we describe our techniques of fully robotic kidney/pancreas transplantation. We trace our evolution from multiport robotics with contralateral graft placement to single-port, ipsilateral implantation through a 5cm incision, preserving the contralateral vessels for a future transplant.
