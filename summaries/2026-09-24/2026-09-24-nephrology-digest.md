### Abstract

Arginine vasopressin resistance (AVP-R), previously called nephrogenic diabetes insipidus is a rare inherited disorder characterized by excessive urine output (often over 10 l/d) and a risk of life-threatening dehydration, resulting from kidney collecting duct insensitivity to arginine vasopressin. Currently, no curative treatment exists. Preclinical studies suggest that repurposing of anti-fungal fluconazole enhances AQP2-mediated water transport.
