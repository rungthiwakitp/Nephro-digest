### Abstract

We thank the authors for their interest and valuable comments regarding our recent publication.1 In our study, we assessed real-world respiratory syncytial virus (RSV) vaccine performance in solid organ transplant recipients (SOTR), a population largely excluded from the initial clinical trials that led to vaccine approval.2 We agree that the study design carries inherent limitations when interpreting the results, particularly regarding data derived from subgroup analyses which are inherently less powered than the main analysis, may have unique sources of confounding, and are susceptible to multiple comparisons.
