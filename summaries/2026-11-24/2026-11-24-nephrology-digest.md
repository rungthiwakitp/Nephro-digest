### Abstract

A 17-year-old girl with no significant medical history underwent abdominal ultrasonography for abdominal pain. Ultrasound revealed multiple renal calcifications and cystic lesions, prompting further evaluation with magnetic resonance imaging (MRI). Both kidneys were normally positioned and of normal size, measuring 116 mm and 117 mm in longitudinal diameter on the right and left, respectively. MRI demonstrated bilateral dilatation of the medullary collecting ducts, predominantly involving the renal pyramids, with multiple bilateral medullary cysts and microcysts (Figure 1).
