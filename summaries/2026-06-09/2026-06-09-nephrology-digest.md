### Abstract

Guidelines play a critical role in translating evidence into consistent, transparent, and actionable recommendations to inform clinical practice, policy, and decision-making. Nevertheless, heterogeneity in guideline development methodology has raised concerns regarding applicability and implementation. In this paper, we describe the structured and systematic guideline development process employed by Kidney Disease: Improving Global Outcomes (KDIGO), which is designed to enhance methodological rigor, transparency, and stakeholder confidence.
