### Abstract

We developed a C57Bl/6 mouse kidney model of cold storage in UW solution prior to transplantation to assess cellular injury during cold ischemic time (CIT). Compared to warm storage, cold storage reduced proximal tubule cell (PTC) death assessed by terminal deoxynucleotidase dUTP nick end labeling (TUNEL). Many PTCs showed increases in nuclear phospho-mixed lineage kinase domain-like protein (pMLKL) with concomitant nuclear swelling and increases in phospho-receptor interacting protein kinase (pRIPK)3, Z-DNA, and Z-DNA binding protein (ZBP)1 but not phospho-RIPK1, caspase 3 cleavage or TUNEL staining.
