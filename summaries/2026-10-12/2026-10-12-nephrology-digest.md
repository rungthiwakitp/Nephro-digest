### Abstract

U.S. organ procurement organizations allocate select deceased donor kidneys “out-of-sequence" (AOOS) by offering a kidney judged at risk of nonuse to a chosen center instead of according to the in-sequence allocation list. In 2025, AOOS practices were scrutinized by the media (February 26: high profile news article) and regulators (August 5: OPTN email deeming AOOS “not consistent with OPTN policy”). Using OPTN data from 1/1/24-2/26/25 (“pre-article”) and 8/6/25-2/28/26 (“post-email”), we studied the association between OPO-level changes in AOOS and kidney transplant activity.
