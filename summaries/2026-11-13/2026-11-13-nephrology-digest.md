### Abstract

Apolipoprotein L1 (APOL1) variants are common in individuals of African descent and increase risk of chronic kidney disease (CKD). Renin-angiotensin system inhibitors (RASis) are a mainstay of therapy for hypertension and proteinuric CKD but were not historically recommended as first-line anti-hypertensive therapy in Black individuals. Here, we evaluated RASis versus other antihypertensive therapy on kidney outcomes in Black individuals by APOL1 genotype.

- Matched keywords: kidney, transplant

### Abstract

Bcl6 is a key transcriptional repressor regulating T cell fate; however, its role in T cell alloimmune responses is largely unknown. We investigated Bcl6 expression in T cells within allografts and the potential of targeting Bcl6 to modulate CD4+ T cell alloactivation. Using mIFISH, we found that Bcl6+CD4+ T cells were highly prevalent in kidney transplant biopsies during rejection. In mixed lymphocyte reactions, pharmacological Bcl6 inhibition (Bcl6i) potently suppressed proliferation and effector cytokine production of human CD4+ T cells in a dose-dependent manner.
