### Abstract

Iptacopan, a factor B inhibitor, targets alternative complement pathway overactivation, a key driver of glomerular inflammation in IgA nephropathy. In the phase 3 APPLAUSE-IgAN trial (NCT04578834), iptacopan significantly slowed kidney function decline versus placebo over 24 months (difference in total estimated glomerular filtration rate [eGFR] slope: 3.02 ml/min per 1.73 m2 per year [95% confidence interval (CI) 2.02, 4.01; P < 0.001]).
