### Abstract

Kidneys from donation after circulatory death (DCD) donors historically had lower recovery and utilization than kidneys from donation after brain death (DBD) donors. However, the organ shortage and advances in organ preservation have increased interest in DCD transplantation. We used OPTN data to study temporal changes in U.S. DCD kidney recovery and transplantation. The percent DCD among kidney donors increased from 21.0% (Era1: 2016-2019) to 30.6% (Era2: 2020-2022) and 42.4% (Era3: 2023-2025).
