### Abstract

Kidney transplantation faces persistent challenges in accurately evaluating donor organ quality and predicting long-term outcomes, with traditional assessment methods demonstrating limited predictive capacity. The increasing utilization of marginal-quality donor organs has intensified the need for sophisticated evaluation strategies. Machine perfusion technology has transformed organ preservation from mere static storage to a dynamic platform potentially enabling real-time monitoring, assessment, and interventions during the preservation period.
