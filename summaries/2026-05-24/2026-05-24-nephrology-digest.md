### Abstract

Acute kidney injury (AKI) occurs in around one third of babies treated in a neonatal intensive care unit (NICU). The establishment of consensus standardized definitions has improved clinical recognition and research into neonatal AKI. Neonatal AKI is now recognized for its independent associations with increased morbidity and NICU mortality. Current definitions utilize increasing serum creatinine and/or decreasing urine output to diagnose AKI, however these biomarkers reflect organ dysfunction long after injury has begun.
