### Abstract

Non-GFR determinants of filtration markers reduce the accuracy of estimated GFR (eGFR) and genetic factors may explain part of this reduced performance. ### Abstract

Tacrolimus remains the backbone of maintenance immunosuppression after kidney transplantation. Once-daily formulations were initially developed to address reduced patient’s drug adherence, a major determinant of poor outcomes. Among the currently available once-daily tacrolimus formulations, the Life-Cycle Pharma Tacrolimus (LCPT) provides improved bioavailability together with a flatter pharmacokinetic profile, characterized by lower peak concentrations than immediate-release formulations1. These pharmacokinetic characteristics may theoretically improve tolerability, reduce intraday variability, and be particularly beneficial in recipients with increased drug clearance, such as CYP3A5 expressers.
