### Abstract

The persistent gap between organ supply and demand remains a critical challenge worldwide. In Taiwan, despite over 630,000 registered organ donors, fewer than 20% of families consent to donation when approached—a phenomenon that underscores the profound disconnect between individual intent and family decision-making during acute grief.1 As of May 2025, 11,183 patients remain on the national transplant waiting list, including 8542 awaiting kidney transplants. This Policy Forum describes the development and implementation of the "Hear My Last Wish" initiative, an innovative information and communication technology (ICT)-based intervention designed to address this challenge by enabling potential donors to record personalized voiceprint messages expressing their donation wishes.
