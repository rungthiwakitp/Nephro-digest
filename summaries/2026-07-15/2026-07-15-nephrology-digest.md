### Abstract

Recently, the STAR group of AST and ASHI published a position on donor-derived cell-free DNA (dd-cfDNA), followed by a letter emphasizing its specificity for rejection and a response. We aim to add personal and methodological perspectives on diagnostic metrics to an ongoing discussion focusing on context of use and clinical utility, of dd-cfDNA across different clinical scenarios. Particularly, we discuss the implications of the overall moderate specificity of published approximately 80%. We argue the transplant community would benefit by shifting descriptive diagnostic metrics to clinical utility within well-defined contexts of use.
