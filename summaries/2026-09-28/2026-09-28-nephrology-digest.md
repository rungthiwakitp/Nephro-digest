### Abstract

Herpes zoster incidence is increased after solid organ transplantation. We analyzed adult recipients of liver, heart, lung, kidney, pancreas or dual-organ transplants between January 2018 and December 2025. We assessed recombinant zoster vaccine uptake and the association between pre-transplant vaccination and post-transplant herpes zoster in 2595 patients. Median follow-up was 2.8 years [IQR 1.6-4.8]. Thirty-two percent of patients completed the two-dose recombinant zoster vaccine series before transplant.
