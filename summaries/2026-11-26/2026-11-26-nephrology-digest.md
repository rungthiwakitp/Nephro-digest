### Abstract

It provides a compelling framework for explaining how embryonic development achieves remarkably reproducible cell identities and tissue organization through processes that are nevertheless dynamic and stochastic. ### Abstract

A 65-year-old man was admitted to the intensive care unit on postoperative day 3 following robot-assisted radical prostatectomy. He presented with dyspnea, abdominal pain, vomiting, and anuria, the latter confirmed by a lack of urine output following Foley catheterization. Laboratory investigations revealed severe acute kidney injury (KDIGO stage 3), with a peak serum creatinine of 684 μmol/L, and medically refractory hyperkalemia (serum potassium, 5.9 mmol/L), prompting urgent hemodialysis in the intensive care unit.
