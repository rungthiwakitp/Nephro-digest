### Abstract

Anti-neutrophil cytoplasmic antibody (ANCA)-associated vasculitides (AAV) often affect the kidneys, driving morbidity and mortality in AAV. Clinical trials have investigated kidney outcomes using a variety of definitions. The standardization of kidney disease response criteria in AAV remains an unmet need, with an urgency to define kidney endpoints for daily clinical practice and new drug development to optimize patient management.A European Vasculitis Society (EUVAS) Focus Group was formed to review the literature and discuss the wide range of definitions used.
