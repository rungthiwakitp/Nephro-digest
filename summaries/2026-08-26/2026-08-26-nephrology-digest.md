### Abstract

Frailty predicts adverse outcomes in kidney transplant candidates, but it remains unclear whether different frailty tools identify the same or distinct at-risk patients. In this retrospective cohort study, 953 kidney transplant candidates listed between 2018 and 2022 underwent frailty assessment using both the FRAIL (Fatigue, Resistance, Ambulation, Illnesses, Weight Loss) scale and Short Physical Performance Battery (SPPB). In a subset with available CT imaging, body composition was analyzed using AI-based tools.
